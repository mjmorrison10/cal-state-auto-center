Our Services

Complete Auto Repair &
Maintenance Under One Roof

From routine oil changes to complex engine diagnostics, Cal-State Auto Center handles it all with skill and honesty. No job too big or too small. We explain your options before we turn a single wrench.

Oil Change & Lube

Quick, affordable oil changes with premium synthetic and conventional oils. We use the exact grade your manufacturer recommends. No unnecessary upsells, just what your car needs.

Brake Service & Repair

Squeaking, grinding, or a spongy pedal? We diagnose the exact problem and show you what needs replacing. Honest brake repairs with no scare tactics. Your safety is our priority.

Tire Sales & Alignment

Great prices on all major tire brands plus precision wheel alignments that extend tire life and improve fuel economy. We price-match any legitimate competitor in Santa Ana.

Engine Repair & Diagnostics

Check engine light on? Our technicians pinpoint the issue with advanced diagnostics and repair it right. From sensors and gaskets to full rebuilds, we keep your engine running strong.

Transmission Service

Hard shifts, slipping, or leaks? We service both automatic and manual transmissions. Get an honest assessment before you spend thousands at the dealer. We save you money.

Electrical & Battery

Dead battery, faulty alternator, or mysterious electrical issues? We trace the root cause, explain it clearly, and fix it right the first time. No guesswork, just results you can count on.

Smog Check & Repair

Need a smog check? We are a certified smog test and repair station. If your car fails, we fix the issue and get you passing fast. One stop, done right.

Suspension & Steering

Bumpy ride, uneven tire wear, or pulling to one side? We diagnose and repair shocks, struts, tie rods, and power steering. Restore your ride quality and stay safe.
